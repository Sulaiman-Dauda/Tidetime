# Glossary

This page explains common Tidetime terms in plain English.

## Admin

A person with permission to manage workspace-wide settings.

## API key

A secret key another app, script, or website can use to connect to Tidetime's API.

## Attendee

The person who books and attends an appointment.

## Availability

The days and times when someone can be booked.

## Blocked period

A date or time range that is closed for bookings even if normal availability says someone is free.

## Booking

A scheduled appointment created when someone chooses a time and finishes the booking flow.

## Booking link

A special link created for a service. It can be limited, temporary, or invite-only.

## Booking page

The public page where people choose a service and book a time.

## Category

A label used to group services together on the booking page.

## Collective scheduling

A team booking style where more than one team member is needed for the same appointment.

## Customer

A person who has booked with you. Tidetime shows customers in a de-duplicated list.

## Destination calendar

The Google Calendar where new bookings should be created after someone books.

## Invite-only link

A booking link meant for a specific person, usually tied to their email address.

## Maintenance mode / Disable public bookings

A workspace setting that pauses public bookings without removing services.

## Owner

The highest-control role in a workspace or team.

## Provider

A team member who delivers the booked service.

## Receptionist

A team role focused on viewing and managing bookings rather than changing system setup.

## Resource

A shared room, tool, piece of equipment, or other asset needed for a booking.

## Review threshold

The minimum rating that counts as positive enough to send someone to your public review page.

## Round-robin scheduling

A team booking style where Tidetime rotates bookings across available team members.

## Self-hosted

Software that you run on your own server or infrastructure instead of relying on a managed cloud service.

## Service

The thing people book, such as a consultation, appointment, class, or session.

## Slot

A single bookable time option, such as 10:00 AM to 10:30 AM.

## Team

A shared workspace where multiple people can collaborate on services and bookings.

## Time zone

The local time region used to show times correctly for users and attendees.

## Webhook

A message Tidetime sends to another app when something happens, such as a booking being created or cancelled.

## Workspace

The Tidetime environment your company or team uses.
