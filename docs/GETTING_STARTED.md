# Getting Started

This guide is for people who want to **use Tidetime**, not install it from scratch.

If Tidetime is not running yet, ask your technical admin to set it up first or use the [deployment guide](./DEPLOYMENT.md).

## What you will do first

Most new users only need to do five things:

1. sign in
2. finish their profile
3. create a service
4. set availability
5. share the booking page

That is enough to start taking bookings.

## 1. Sign in or create the owner account

### If you are setting up a brand-new Tidetime workspace

The very first person will see a setup screen instead of a normal login page.

Use it to create the **owner account** for the workspace.

After that, Tidetime takes you straight into the main app so you can create your first service.

### If Tidetime has already been set up

- open your Tidetime web address
- sign in with your email and password
- if you do not have an account yet, ask the workspace owner or team admin to invite you

## 2. Complete your profile

Open your account menu and go to **Profile settings**, then review:

- your name
- username
- short bio
- time zone
- time format
- week start day
- password

Why this matters:

- your **username** is part of your public booking link
- your **bio** appears on your booking page
- your **time zone** helps Tidetime show times correctly for you

## 3. Create your first service

Go to **Dashboard → Services** and choose **Create your first service**.

A service is the thing people book with you, such as:

- Intro call
- Hair appointment
- Consultation
- Class
- Team session

Start simple. For your first service, focus on these fields:

- **Title** — what people are booking
- **URL slug** — the link part for that service
- **Description** — a short explanation of what the service is for
- **Default length** — how long it lasts
- **Location** — video link, phone call, in-person, or another option

You can also decide whether the service should:

- stay visible on your public page
- require confirmation before it is fully booked
- allow payment
- ask custom booking questions

> Tip: do not try to perfect every option on day one. Create a simple service first, then improve it after a test booking.

## 4. Set your availability

Go to **Dashboard → Availability**.

This is where you tell Tidetime when you are available for bookings.

You can set:

- your regular weekly hours
- special one-off date changes
- days when you are unavailable

A good starting point is to add your normal working hours first.

Example:

- Monday to Friday
- 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM

You can always tighten this later with:

- shorter windows
- breaks
- date overrides
- blocked periods managed by an admin

## 5. Preview your booking page

After creating a service, open **Dashboard → Services**.

From there you can preview the public booking page for that service.

Check that:

- the title looks right
- the description is clear
- the duration is correct
- the location is correct
- the available times make sense

If something looks off, go back and adjust the service or your availability.

## 6. Share your booking link

There are two common ways to share Tidetime:

### Share your public page

This is usually the easiest option.

It lets people see your visible services in one place.

### Share a direct service link

Use this when you want to send people straight to one service only.

Examples:

- a consultation link in your email signature
- a special service link on your website
- a direct link in a message to a customer

You can also use **Dashboard → Booking Links** if you want a special link that is:

- single-use
- invite-only
- time-limited
- limited to a set number of bookings

## 7. Take a test booking

Before sharing Tidetime widely, make one test booking yourself.

This helps you confirm:

- the right times are showing
- the booking form is easy to understand
- confirmations and reminders behave as expected
- the booking appears in your dashboard
- Google Calendar and payments work if you enabled them

## 8. Optional next steps

Once the basics are working, you can improve your setup with:

- **Google Calendar** in **Settings → Calendar**
- **email sending** in **Settings → Email**
- **Stripe payments** in **Settings → Stripe**
- **reviews** in **Settings → Reviews**
- **teams** in **Dashboard → Teams**
- **resources** in **Dashboard → Resources**
- **categories** in **Dashboard → Categories**

If you are the workspace owner or admin, continue with the [Admin Guide](./ADMIN_GUIDE.md).

## First-day checklist

Use this checklist to get live quickly:

- [ ] sign in successfully
- [ ] complete your profile settings
- [ ] create at least one service
- [ ] set weekly availability
- [ ] preview your booking page
- [ ] make one test booking
- [ ] share your link
